Atherogenic Dyslipidemia

An imbalance in bloodstream fats: excessive "bad" cholesterol (LDL) and triglycerides with insufficient protective fats, increasing plaque accumulation risk in arterial walls.

Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c)

A 90-day time-capsule reflecting the average percentage of sugar-coated red blood cells. Values between 5.7% and 6.4% represent early metabolic prediabetes.
