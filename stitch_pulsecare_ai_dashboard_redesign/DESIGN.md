## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a clinical-grade medical intelligence interface rooted in Swiss modernist rigor and Scandinavian clarity. The visual ethos communicates absolute diagnostic veracity, calm computational authority, and crystalline legibility. Designed for clinicians, laboratory researchers, and medical specialists operating under high cognitive load, the interface reduces anxiety through generous negative space, pristine white surfaces, and measured typographic scale.

The aesthetic philosophy balances clinical minimalism with high-precision technological affordances:
- **Atmospheric Clarity**: Luminous, airy alabaster canvases with deliberate structural grouping rather than heavy visual framing.
- **Architectural Rigor**: High-density diagnostic telemetry organized via strict metric grids, subdued structural dividers, and razor-sharp typographic alignments.
- **Controlled Luminescence**: Restrained application of luminous cerulean cyan accents to focus cognitive attention solely on active diagnostic pathways, anomalous markers, and actionable inferences.
- **Surgical Tactility**: Translucent frosted overlays and gossamer-fine borders create physical separation without introducing visual mass or visual fatigue.

## Colors

The color system is organized around diagnostic legibility, strict physiological categorization, and absolute contrast compliance (WCAG AAA for all clinical metric readouts).

### Base & Canvas Surfaces
- **Canvas Base**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure clinical stark white for primary workspace cards, focal surfaces, and data sheets)
- **Canvas Substrate**: `#F8FAFC` (Alabaster neutral used for outer viewport backdrops and structural canvas framing)
- **Surface Recessed**: `#F1F5F9` (Muted cool foundation for nested data panels, table headers, and structural wells)

### Structural Grayscale & Typography
- **Text Primary / Deep Slate**: `#0F172A` (Maximum contrast for clinical headlines, primary metrics, and acute findings)
- **Text Secondary / Navy Slate**: `#334155` (Secondary diagnostic copy, data point descriptions, and axis markers)
- **Text Muted / Cool Slate**: `#64748B` (De-emphasized labels, clinical metadata, timestamps, and inactive units)
- **Border Subtle**: `#E2E8F0` (Gossamer structural boundaries, table horizontal dividers, and quiet card edges)
- **Border Strong**: `#CBD5E1` (Active card framing, input boundaries, and high-emphasis segment splitters)

### Diagnostic Semantics & Accents
- **Primary / Diagnostic Cyan**: `#0284C7` (Default core actions, AI synthesis tags, active diagnostic states; paired with `#0EA5E9` for focus highlights and hover fills)
- **Clinical Emerald (Optimal / Normal)**: `#059669` (Target-range lab results, physiological stability, verified inferences; paired with `#10B981` for data visualization series)
- **Clinical Amber (Elevated / Caution)**: `#D97706` (Borderline vitals, pending review states, intermediate risk stratification; paired with `#F59E0B` for chart alerts)
- **Critical Coral (Acute / Urgent)**: `#E11D48` (Immediate clinical hazards, critical lab deltas, emergent intervention signals; paired with `#EF4444` for active pulse beacons)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances structural precision with effortless readability. **Outfit** serves as the display and metric typeface, lending geometric order and crisp numerical presence to diagnostic summaries and core vitals. **Plus Jakarta Sans** functions as the primary operational text face, optimized for rapid scanning of pathological records, clinical narratives, and granular laboratory parameters.

### Implementation Rules
- All numeric readouts, unit metrics, and biomarker values utilize proportional tabular lining figures to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time streaming updates.
- Micro-labels and anatomical status indicators leverage uppercase transformation strictly when paired with `label-sm` and letter spacing of `0.04em`.
- Never compress line heights below the specified values; diagnostic safety requires ample interlinear clearance to avoid confusion between overlapping prescription rows and multi-line medical notations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to a rigorous 8px spatial grid, enforcing structured discipline while maintaining an expansive Scandinavian airiness. Diagnostic workloads require modular density without physical claustrophobia.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1440px and above)**: 12-column fluid grid with `margin-lg` (48px) margins and `gutter-lg` (32px) gutters. Ideal for split-pane diagnostic workflows: a primary 8-column clinical dossier paired with a persistent 4-column AI differential panel.
- **Tablet / Workstation (768px – 1439px)**: 8-column layout with `margin` (32px) margins and `gutter` (24px) gutters. Complex laboratory tables collapse into progressive disclosure cards.
- **Mobile Handheld (<768px)**: 4-column layout with `margin-sm` (16px) margins and `gutter-sm` (16px) gutters. Sticky bottom actions replace floating inline controls.

### Density and Grouping
- Internal card padding defaults to `space-lg` (24px) for focal diagnostic summaries and drops to `space-md` (16px) inside dense data grids and metric monitors.
- Related data pairs (such as a biomarker label and its associated reading) maintain a strict `space-xs` (4px) relationship to preserve immediate Gestalt proximity.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system rejects heavy drop shadows in favor of pristine Scandinavian optical physics: translucence, soft daylight diffusion, and hairline geometric separation.

### Surface Tiers & Translucency
- **Substrate Level 0**: Raw `#F8FAFC` background canvas with zero elevation.
- **Clinical Card Level 1**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px hairline border in `#E2E8F0`, underpinned by a soft ambient daylight shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Diagnostic Focus Level 2**: Interactive or hovering panels; shadow amplifies slightly to `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with border shifting to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Floating Overlays & AI Popovers Level 3**: Frosted glassmorphism using `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, a crisp perimeter of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)`, and an ultra-diffused floor shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The interface utilizes a disciplined `roundedness: 2` scale (8px base corner radius), achieving an organic, humane quality without surrendering scientific authority.

- **Micro Controls & Badges**: 6px (`rounded-md`) for biometric tags, micro status pills, and numeric delta badges.
- **Standard Controls & Inputs**: 8px (`rounded-lg`) for form inputs, interactive buttons, contextual popovers, and table rows.
- **Structural Panels & Clinical Cards**: 16px (`rounded-xl`) for comprehensive diagnostic modules, patient overview cards, and laboratory charting canvases.
- **Pill Shape Exception**: Dedicated roundness (`9999px`) is reserved solely for live telemetry pills, patient triage status pills, and discrete category filters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Clinical Action**: Background `#0284C7`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius 8px, font weight 600. Resting shadow `0 1px 2px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.20)`. Hover state transitions to `#0EA5E9` with subtle vertical translation (-0.5px).
- **Secondary / Review**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover transitions background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Critical Action**: Background `#E11D48`, text `#FFFFFF`. Used strictly for irreversible clinical overrides or emergency order dispatch.

### Status Chips & Biomarker Badges
- Status badges feature a two-tone construction: an ultra-subtle tinted surface fill (8% opacity of the semantic color), paired with deep, high-contrast semantic typography and a matching 6px circular indicator dot.
- **Optimal / Normal**: Background `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.08)`, text `#059669`.
- **Elevated / Warning**: Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`, text `#D97706`.
- **Critical / Urgent**: Background `rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.08)`, text `#E11D48`.

### Input Fields & Select Controls
- Height fixed at 40px for desktop precision. Background `#FFFFFF`, perimeter border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#64748B`.
- Focused state applies an outer focus ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)` with border color shifting to `#0284C7`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Custom 18px x 18px geometry with 4px corner radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Checked: `#0284C7` solid fill with crisp white vector iconography.

### Clinical Metric Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, and 16px border-radius.
- Cards maintain distinct anatomical zones: a top label row (`label-sm` in `#64748B`), an oversized metric value (`metric-display` in `#0F172A`), followed by an inline baseline deviation indicator (e.g., `+2.4% vs baseline`) rendered using semantic emerald, amber, or rose tokens.

### Specialized AI Differential Ingestion Panel
- A distinct glassmorphic card incorporating `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)` fill and `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.
- Left structural border features a vertical 3px accent line in `#0284C7` to clearly demarcate machine-synthesized diagnostic interpretations from human laboratory inputs.
